How does the Hard AI play?
  1. Never hands you a box early. While there are "safe" lines (ones that don't put a third side on a box), it only plays those.
  2. Searches to the end. Once the board is small enough, it tries every possible rest of the game using negamax with alpha-beta pruning. It skips any line that can't beat its best so far, and a transposition table remembers positions it has already solved.
  3. Plays like an expert without being told how. The search finds the long chain rule and double-dealing on its own. Sometimes it leaves you the last two boxes of a chain on purpose, so you're forced to open the next, longer one.

Easy grabs boxes but plays anywhere else. Medium plays safe lines, then gives away the smallest chain it can.
